One job per hospital, kind and period (`scheduled_job`); each job and its effects commit in one transaction under `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, which gives a lease and fencing token's guarantees because all the work is in the database; failures roll back and retry with backoff, then are marked failed and audited. ## Open decisions for InnovaStat and the hospital

- Thresholds: disclosure cell size (default 5), minimum patients per arm (30), FDR level (0.05), high-burden share (5%).
- Analyses, gaps, the study workbench and draft reports are visible only to InnovaStat roles; the hospital sees released reports, the ideas in them and sent manuscripts.
- Whether the Claude agent may be enabled, and under which data-processing terms. The patient-level upload itself conflicts with the handoff's pilot scope ("patient-level records… stay in the hospital-approved environment"), so the data-sharing agreement must explicitly allow it.
- Multi-sheet workbooks (joining admissions, labs and costs) and QALY-based economic models are not built yet.
- Arabic translations beyond navigation labels: RTL layout is in place, but the terminology needs hospital sign-off.
- Hosting: deploy as InnovaEvidence does (single Ubuntu server or Render). The deploy scripts are not yet adapted.
